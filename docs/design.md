# Design Direction

- **Premise:** a rolling clay ball traverses a narrow diorama strip; the character, terrain edge, and deep dirt underside share the frame.
- **Material language:** hand-sculpted plasticine/claymation — matte, bright flat colour, fingerprint texture, soft irregular forms. Imperfection should look deliberate, not procedural noise for its own sake.
- **Shapes:** rounded, lumpy, sagging/overhanging grass; avoid sharp, sterile, grid-perfect geometry.
- **Presentation:** camera follows the player from a raised rear/side view, preserving the grass/dirt cutoff. Warm ambient lighting supports the material read; avoid glossy highlights.
- **Player:** the face is an animated clay feature riding on a physically readable rolling body, not a rigid decal. Its return to a resting orientation makes the character feel alive.
- **Motion:** landing leaves a visible plastic set—compression/bulge holds while idle. Rolling gradually remolds it toward a slightly rounder, still hand-made shape; never use an elastic squash-and-rebound or a perfect sphere.

Before changing visual language or camera framing, validate it in the running scene rather than optimizing only for mesh cleanliness.
